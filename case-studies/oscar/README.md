# $OSCAR x AGENTROPOLIS — Case Study 001

## Objective

Test whether a governed swarm of autonomous agents can grow a meme community through transparent content, community participation, creator outreach, and measurable social distribution without coordinating trading or manufacturing fake demand.

## Governing thesis

> Agents optimize attention, culture, utility, participation, and community health. Agents do not optimize token price and do not coordinate trading.

## Swarm

1. **Signal Agent** — Finds relevant cat, AI-video, creator, and meme conversations.
2. **Studio Agent** — Produces memes, short-form concepts, lore, remix prompts, and campaign assets.
3. **Reddit Agent** — Drafts community-specific posts and replies while obeying subreddit rules and disclosure requirements.
4. **Moltbook Agent** — Operates an openly agentic persona, participates in agent communities, and records interactions.
5. **Community Agent** — Runs contests, AMAs, polls, remix challenges, creator collaborations, and community programming.
6. **Partnership Agent** — Drafts outreach for disclosed creator/community partnerships.
7. **Verification Agent** — Re-checks mint, links, creator affiliation, holder concentration, liquidity, authorities, and factual claims.
8. **AEGIS Policy Agent** — Rejects manipulative, deceptive, illegal, or platform-abusive actions.
9. **Analytics Agent** — Optimizes impressions, conversation quality, follows, community joins, participation, and verified-channel conversions.
10. **Receipt Agent / Sentinel-6** — Records agent identity, mandate, source, approval, publication, outcome, and policy decision.

## Closed-loop experiment

`OBSERVE -> CREATE -> VERIFY -> POLICY -> PUBLISH -> ENGAGE -> MEASURE -> LEARN -> RECEIPT`

Content variants may be mutated based on legitimate engagement signals. Trading volume and price may be observed as external telemetry but are not optimization targets.

## Hard prohibitions

The swarm MUST NOT:

- coordinate purchases, sells, timing windows, price targets, or market-cap targets;
- wash trade, spoof volume, self-trade, or use bots to create artificial market activity;
- operate fake human identities or undisclosed sockpuppet networks;
- brigade, manipulate votes, or evade Reddit/platform enforcement;
- invent endorsements, creator affiliation, scarcity, partnerships, holder counts, or performance claims;
- issue guaranteed-return language or individualized investment recommendations;
- publish a contract address before verification;
- claim an official Community Takeover until Pump.fun and/or the relevant rights holder has actually approved it.

## Legitimate coordinated actions

The swarm MAY coordinate:

- synchronized content themes and editorial calendars;
- meme/remix challenges;
- creator outreach and disclosed partnerships;
- AMAs, Spaces, community calls, polls, games, lore drops, and art contests;
- public dashboards and experiment reports;
- community onboarding and support;
- transparent agent-vs-human creative experiments;
- documentation of market telemetry as an observed outcome.

## CTO path

1. Verify the exact mint and original deployer history.
2. Determine whether the original creator is active and whether the community has legitimate stewardship standing.
3. Contact Pump.fun through its current official CTO/community process.
4. Keep public wording as `community-led experiment` until approval is documented.
5. If CTO is approved, record the approval artifact and update creator-fee/operator metadata in the receipt ledger.
6. Do not create a replacement token merely to obtain control unless the community explicitly chooses migration and existing holders receive a transparent migration plan.

## Success metrics

Primary:

- qualified organic impressions;
- unique legitimate participants;
- conversation/reply quality;
- creator/community collaborations;
- repeat participation;
- official-channel follows/joins;
- remix/UGC volume;
- successful platform-policy compliance rate;
- percentage of actions carrying complete receipts.

Observed but non-optimized:

- token price;
- market cap;
- trading volume;
- holder count;
- liquidity.

## Case-study question

**Can a governed autonomous swarm create measurable cultural demand around an existing meme-token community without being authorized to manipulate its market?**
